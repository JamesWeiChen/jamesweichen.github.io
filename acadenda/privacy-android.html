Privacy Policy for Acadenda for Android

Effective date: 8 October 2026
Developer: Wei James Chen
Privacy contact: thisis.chenwei@gmail.com

Local conference information

Acadenda is a conference agenda and note-taking app. You do not need an Acadenda account. Imported agendas, saved map images, favorites, talk and person notes, research interests, followed people, timer settings, reminder preferences, and local support history are stored on your device.

The app uses this information to display your agenda, match talks to your research interests locally, keep your notes, and provide timers and reminders. These local notes and research profiles are not sent to an Acadenda server. This Android version has no cloud synchronization, advertising service, or developer-operated analytics service.

Files, QR codes and notifications

The app reads agenda files or images you choose through Android's file picker. If you choose QR scanning, camera access is used to read the code locally; camera frames are not uploaded by Acadenda. A scanned download link is handled as described below.

Optional notifications provide reminders for starred conference events. Reminder scheduling uses the conference timezone and Android notification settings. You can disable notifications or camera access in Android settings. The app does not request device location or address-book access.

Downloads you request

When you import an agenda or download a map from a URL, or choose to check a conference for updates, the app connects to the server at that URL. Download connections use HTTPS. The receiving server can see normal connection information such as your IP address, request time, and the requested path or query parameters, including information already contained in the URL.

The app does not add your local notes or research profile to these requests. The operator of the selected server controls its own logs and retention; consult that operator's privacy policy. Choose links from sources you trust.

Optional purchases through Google Play

All app features are free. Support Us provides optional, repeatable one-time purchases through Google Play. Google handles checkout, account/payment information and purchase processing under its own policies. Acadenda does not receive your full payment-card details.

The app receives purchase data from Google Play, including the product, purchase time and state, purchase token, signature, and available order information. It uses purchase data to verify and process the purchase, prevent duplicate local records, and consume the product so it can be purchased again. A local support record is retained on the device. Pending transaction data is kept locally while processing remains incomplete.

Google services may also process service-related technical data according to their policies. Refer to the Google Privacy Policy. Deleting local app data does not delete Google's transaction records or refund a purchase. Consumed support purchases are not restored as a cross-device history by this app.

Retention, deletion and security

Local app data remains on the device until you remove it through app controls, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes local notes and support history. This version does not provide cloud recovery or note export; keep any original import files you need separately. Android app backup and app-data transfer are disabled by the app configuration.

App data is stored in the app's private storage. The app uses Android's standard HTTPS certificate checks for network downloads and Google Play for checkout. Device access, operating-system protections, and third-party services also affect data security.

Contact and changes

If you contact the developer by email, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to include, for the purpose of answering your request. Avoid including payment-card details, passwords, or unnecessary personal information. Contact the privacy address above with questions about this policy or requests concerning correspondence you have sent.

The developer will update this policy when the app's data practices change and display the effective date in the published policy.

Acadenda Android 隱私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 8 日
開發者：Wei James Chen
隱私聯絡信箱：thisis.chenwei@gmail.com

本機議程與筆記

Acadenda 是研討會議程與筆記應用程式，不需要建立 Acadenda 帳號。 匯入的議程、儲存的地圖圖片、收藏、報告與人物筆記、研究興趣、 追蹤人物、計時器設定、提醒偏好及支持紀錄，均儲存在你的裝置上。

這些資料用於顯示議程、在本機比對報告與研究興趣、保存筆記， 以及提供計時器與提醒。本機筆記與研究資料不會傳送至 Acadenda 伺服器。此 Android 版本沒有雲端同步、廣告服務或開發者自行營運 的使用分析服務。

檔案、QR code 與通知

App 透過 Android 檔案選擇器讀取你選取的議程檔案或圖片。 選擇 QR 掃描時，相機僅用於本機辨識代碼，Acadenda 不會上傳 相機影像。掃描所得的下載連結依下方「下載」說明處理。

選用的通知功能會提醒已收藏的會議活動，排程依會議時區及 Android 通知設定處理。你可以在 Android 設定中停用通知或相機權限。 App 不要求裝置定位或通訊錄存取權限。

你主動要求的下載

透過網址匯入議程、下載地圖或自行檢查議程更新時，App 會連線至 該網址的伺服器，下載連線使用 HTTPS。接收端可能看見一般連線資訊， 例如 IP 位址、請求時間、網址路徑及查詢參數，包括網址本身已有的資訊。

App 不會在這些請求中加入你的本機筆記或研究資料。 所選伺服器的營運者自行決定紀錄與保存方式，請參閱對方的隱私政策， 並使用你信任的來源。

Google Play 自願支持購買

所有 App 功能免費。Support Us 提供可重複購買的一次性自願支持項目。 Google 依其政策處理結帳、帳號、付款資料及交易；Acadenda 不會取得 完整信用卡資料。

App 從 Google Play 接收商品、購買時間與狀態、交易 token、簽章及 可取得的訂單資訊，用於驗證與處理購買、防止重複新增本機紀錄， 以及消耗商品以供再次購買。本機支持紀錄保存在裝置上，尚未完成 處理的交易資料會在本機保留至處理完成。

Google 服務也可能依其政策處理服務相關技術資料，請參閱 Google 隱私政策。 刪除 App 的本機資料不會刪除 Google 的交易紀錄，也不會自動退款。 此 App 不提供已消耗支持項目的跨裝置歷史恢復。

保存、刪除與安全

本機資料會保留至你透過 App 功能移除、在 Android 設定中清除 App 儲存空間，或解除安裝。清除儲存空間或解除安裝會移除本機筆記及支持紀錄。 此版本沒有雲端恢復或筆記匯出功能；需要保留的原始匯入檔案請自行保存。 App 設定已停用 Android 應用程式備份與 App 資料轉移。

資料保存在 App 私有儲存空間。下載使用 Android 標準 HTTPS 憑證驗證， 結帳使用 Google Play。裝置存取、作業系統保護及第三方服務， 也會影響資料安全。

聯絡與政策更新

你主動以電子郵件聯絡開發者時，開發者會收到你的信箱及自行提供的內容， 用於回覆你的問題。請避免寄送信用卡資料、密碼或不必要的個人資訊。 對本政策有疑問，或需要處理你曾寄送的聯絡內容，請使用上方隱私聯絡信箱。

App 的資料處理方式改變時，開發者會更新本政策， 並在公開政策中標示生效日期。
