Privacy Policy for Beamer Notes for Android

Effective date: 15 October 2026
Developer: Wei James Chen
Application ID: com.beamernotes.app
Privacy contact: thisis.chenwei@gmail.com

This policy describes Beamer Notes for Android version 1.26 (4). The Apple version has a separate privacy policy.

PDFs, annotations and settings

Beamer Notes reads PDFs you select through Android's document picker and copies them into private app storage. The app processes the PDFs on your device to display slides and speaker notes. It stores your library titles and folders, PDF content hashes, drawing strokes, inserted text and shapes, and presentation preferences locally. Content hashes identify imported PDFs; they are not advertising identifiers.

The developer does not receive these PDFs, notes, annotations or hashes. The current Android version has no developer-operated PDF cloud synchronization, advertising, tracking, or separate analytics or crash-reporting service. If you choose a file from a cloud document provider, that provider handles your file access under its own policy.

External displays and Google Cast

Connecting an external display shows your audience presentation on that display. When you choose a Google Cast receiver, the app sends rendered audience slide images and drawing or laser-pointer updates to the selected receiver. It does not send the original PDF or the private speaker-notes pane through the app's Cast channel. People who can see or record the receiving screen can see the displayed content. Disconnect Cast to end the session.

Google Cast uses Google Play services. Google's Cast SDK disclosure describes collection of discovery and session events, mobile-device information, client-app and SDK metadata, and technical usage data. The SDK sends encrypted diagnostics to Google to improve reliability and performance. Google describes these logs as anonymous and aggregated, with short-term raw retention. The app offers no separate SDK-data opt-out or deletion control. See Google's Cast data disclosure and Google Privacy Policy.

A Cast receiver also loads its receiver web app and Google's Cast framework over the network. Hosting and service providers can receive normal web-request information, such as IP address, request time, and browser/device information. The app does not upload your PDF library to the developer as part of casting.

Accounts and purchases

Account sign-in, purchases and server-managed PDF allowances are not enabled in this Android release. Local PDF use is not limited by a purchase allowance. The app does not send Google sign-in tokens, purchase tokens or PDF hashes to an account or credit server in this release. It does not offer cross-install purchase/allowance recovery, PDF cloud sync or phone remote control. If these data practices change in a future release, this policy will be updated.

Device access, backup and deletion

The app uses the files you select and network access for Cast and links you choose to open. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts or precise location. Files and settings are protected by Android's app-private storage and device security.

Android backup and device-to-device transfer are enabled for eligible local app data. Depending on your device and backup settings, PDFs, annotations and settings may be copied or restored by Android or your backup provider. This is an operating-system service, not Beamer Notes PDF cloud sync. The app excludes its account and purchase preference files from backup and transfer.

Local data remains until you delete it using app controls, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. Removing a deck removes its local PDF and associated annotations. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes the local app data; original files outside the app and backup copies are separate. Manage or delete backups through your device or backup provider. The developer cannot access or remotely delete content stored only on your devices or in your backup account.

Website links and contacting the developer

Opening the developer website or this policy connects your browser to the website host. This policy page uses no analytics scripts, advertising or forms. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process ordinary connection logs under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you email the developer, the developer and email service providers receive your email address and the information you choose to send to answer your request. Do not send passwords, payment details, or private PDFs unless needed for your request. Correspondence is retained for handling support and any applicable legal obligations; you may request access or deletion through the privacy contact above. The developer does not sell your app content or use it for advertising.

Children and policy updates

The app does not ask children to provide personal information or create an account. The Google-service and backup processing described above can still apply when the app is used by a child. Contact the developer with concerns about information a child has sent by email.

Changes to the app's data practices will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Contact the privacy address above with questions about data handling, retention or deletion.

Beamer Notes Android 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 15 日
開發者：Wei James Chen
應用程式 ID：com.beamernotes.app
隱私聯絡信箱：thisis.chenwei@gmail.com

本政策適用於 Beamer Notes Android 1.26 (4)。Apple 版本另有專用隱私權政策。

PDF、筆跡與設定

Beamer Notes 透過 Android 文件選擇器讀取你選取的 PDF，並複製到 App 私有儲存空間。PDF 在裝置上處理，用於顯示投影片與講者筆記。App 在本機保存文件庫標題與資料夾、PDF 內容雜湊值、繪圖筆跡、插入的文字與圖形，以及簡報偏好。雜湊值用於識別匯入的 PDF，並非廣告識別碼。

開發者不會收到這些 PDF、筆記、註記或雜湊值。目前 Android 版沒有開發者營運的 PDF 雲端同步、廣告、追蹤或獨立的使用分析與當機回報服務。若從雲端文件供應商選取檔案，該供應商依自身政策處理檔案存取。

外接顯示器與 Google Cast

連接外接顯示器時，App 會在該螢幕顯示觀眾簡報。選取 Google Cast 接收器時，App 將觀眾投影片的繪製影像，以及筆跡或雷射筆更新傳送至你選取的接收器。App 的 Cast 通道不傳送原始 PDF 或私有的講者筆記區。能看見或錄製接收螢幕的人可以看見顯示內容；中斷 Cast 即結束連線。

Google Cast 使用 Google Play 服務。Google 的 Cast SDK 說明指出，SDK 會收集裝置探索與工作階段事件、行動裝置資訊、App 與 SDK 中繼資料及技術使用資料，並將加密診斷傳送給 Google，以改善穩定性及效能。Google 說明這些紀錄採匿名、彙整方式處理，原始紀錄僅短期保存。App 沒有另行退出或刪除 SDK 資料的控制項。請參閱 Google Cast 資料揭露及 Google 隱私權政策。

Cast 接收器也會透過網路載入接收端網頁與 Google Cast framework。主機及服務供應商可能收到一般網頁請求資訊，例如 IP 位址、請求時間與瀏覽器或裝置資訊。投放時，App 不會將 PDF 文件庫上傳給開發者。

帳號與購買

此 Android 版本尚未啟用帳號登入、購買或伺服器 PDF 額度功能，本機 PDF 使用不受購買額度限制。此版本不會將 Google 登入 token、購買 token 或 PDF 雜湊值傳送至帳號或額度伺服器，也未提供跨重裝購買或額度還原、PDF 雲端同步或手機遙控。未來版本若改變資料處理方式，將更新本政策。

裝置存取、備份與刪除

App 使用你選取的檔案，並透過網路提供 Cast 及開啟你選取的連結。App 不要求相機、麥克風、通訊錄或精確定位權限。檔案及設定受 Android App 私有儲存空間及裝置安全機制保護。

符合條件的本機 App 資料可使用 Android 備份與裝置間轉移。依你的裝置及備份設定，Android 或備份供應商可能複製或還原 PDF、筆跡及設定。這是作業系統服務，並非 Beamer Notes PDF 雲端同步。App 已將帳號及購買偏好檔案排除於備份與轉移之外。

本機資料會保存至你透過 App 刪除、清除 App 儲存空間或解除安裝。刪除文件會移除該本機 PDF 與相關註記；清除儲存空間或解除安裝會移除本機 App 資料。App 外的原始檔案及備份副本需另行管理。請透過裝置或備份供應商管理、刪除備份。開發者無法存取或遠端刪除僅存在於你的裝置或備份帳號中的內容。

網站連結與聯絡開發者

開啟開發者網站或本政策時，瀏覽器會連線至網站主機。本政策網頁沒有分析程式、廣告或表單，由 GitHub Pages 託管；GitHub 可能依 GitHub 隱私權聲明處理一般連線紀錄。

你以電子郵件聯絡開發者時，開發者與郵件服務供應商會收到你的信箱及自行提供的內容，用於處理問題。除非處理問題所需，請勿寄送密碼、付款資料或私人 PDF。聯絡紀錄用於客服處理及適用的法律義務；可透過上方隱私信箱要求存取或刪除。開發者不販售你的 App 內容，也不將其用於廣告。

兒童與政策更新

App 不要求兒童提供個人資訊或建立帳號；兒童使用時，前述 Google 服務與備份處理仍可能適用。若對兒童透過電子郵件提供的資訊有疑慮，請聯絡開發者。

App 的資料處理方式變更時，本頁會更新並標示新的生效日期。對資料處理、保存或刪除有疑問，請使用上方隱私聯絡信箱。
